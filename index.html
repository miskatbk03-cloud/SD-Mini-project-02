Our story

Food Mood was founded in 2018 by owner Miskat BK, starting with a single charcoal grill and a family spice box. Today our dining room keeps that same warmth: low amber lights, wooden tables and an open kitchen where you can watch the fire work.

Our vegetables and herbs come from farms within 50 km, and our spices are toasted and ground in-house every morning. Chef Rahim Uddin's signature is the smoked lamb shank, slow-cooked for eight hours over mango wood.
